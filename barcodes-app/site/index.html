Frequently asked questions

How do I add a barcode?
Tap the + button, then either scan a barcode with your camera or type the value in by hand.

The scanner isn't working.
Make sure Barcodes has camera access in Settings → Barcodes → Camera. You can also add any barcode manually instead of scanning.

How do I show a barcode to a scanner?
Tap a saved barcode to open it full-screen. The screen brightness is boosted automatically so checkout and gate scanners can read it.

Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your device. See the privacy policy below.

Barcodes requires iOS 17 or later.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Barcodes is designed to be private. The app stores everything locally on your device and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Barcodes has no account and no sign-in. The barcodes you save — their titles and values — are stored only on your device using Apple's on-device storage. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Camera

Barcodes uses your device's camera solely to detect and read barcodes when you choose to scan one. Camera processing happens on your device in real time. Images from the camera are not saved, uploaded, or shared.

Network and tracking

Barcodes makes no network requests. There are no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs. Because nothing leaves your device, there is no data for us to access, sell, or disclose.

Data deletion

Your saved barcodes remain under your control. Delete an individual barcode from within the app, or remove all data by deleting the app from your device.

Children's privacy

Barcodes does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
