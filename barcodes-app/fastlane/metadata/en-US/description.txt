Barcodes is the simplest way to save the codes you use every day and pull them up in an instant.

Scan a loyalty card, gym pass, library card, or membership badge with your camera—or type the value in by hand—and Barcodes keeps it safe. When you need it, tap to display the barcode full-screen at full brightness so any scanner can read it cleanly.

FEATURES
• Scan barcodes with your camera using fast, on-device recognition
• Add codes manually for anything you can't scan
• Full-screen display with an automatic brightness boost for reliable scanning
• Edit titles and values anytime
• Clean, simple, and quick to use

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything is stored locally on your iPhone. Barcodes has no account, no sign-in, no ads, and no tracking. Your data never leaves your device.

Lighten your wallet—save your barcodes and leave the cards at home.
